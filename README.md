# Customer Shopping Behavior Insights - Project Overview
## The goal of this project is to determine how the company can leverage customer shopping behavior data to improve customer engagement and optimise product and marketing strategies.
GlobalSales is a company using e-commerce and physical retail stores to sell apparel, footwear, outerwear, and accessories nationwide via website, mobile app, and in-store pickup. The company has significant amounts of data on its sales, marketing efforts, operational efficiency, product offerings, and loyalty program that has been previously underutilised. This project thoroughly analyses and synthesises this data to uncover critical insights that will improve the store’s commercial success.

## Insights Summary
#### To evaluate customer behavior performance, we focused on the following key metrics:

- **Sales**: Total money you made from orders.
- **AOV**: How much each person spends per order on average.
- **Orders**: How many times people bought.

## Product Team

#### Sales
-Across categories, Clothing ($102.97k) and Accessories ($73.48k) are the top sellers, responsible for 76.5% of sales. Across categories, Outerwear ($18.4k) is the worst performer, responsible for ~8% of sales.
- Across clothing, Blouse, shirt and dress are the biggest contributors to this increase in sales, generating $10.3k, $ 10.24k, and $10.2k, respectively, while jeans is the worst performer ($7.55k). Across accessories, jewellery and belts are the biggest contributors to this increase, bringing in 9.9k and 9.52k, respectively.
-Across the worst performer in the category, it only has two products: jacket ($9.25k) and coat ($9.15k), which is lower compared to the other categories, which sell more than 3 products.

#### AOV

-Across categories, Footwear is the best performer by AOV at $60.23, which is more than the average of $59.7. The best performers in sales, Clothing ($59.93) and Accessories ($59.79), rank lower than Footwear when compared by AOV. Outerwear ($7.15) is the worst performer and accounts for 24.1% of overall AOV.
- Across Footwear, boots ($62.36) and shoes ($61.88) are the biggest contributors to this surge in AOV, while sandals ($57.41) are the worst performers.
- Across products, T-shirt and dress across clothing and Scarf and Gary across accessories are responsible for the major increase in AOV.
-Across Outerwear, jacket ($56.74) is the worst performer in AOV, which is lower than that of all other products.

#### Orders

- Surprisingly, the best performers in sales, clothing (574) and accessories (522), are the best performers in orders generated across the category. And the worst performer in sales, Outerwear (239 orders), is also the worst performer in generating orders, which is responsible for 37.22% of the orders.
- Across products, pants (149) and blouses (147) across clothing and jewellery (144) and sunglasses (143) across accessories are responsible for many of the higher orders across the two categories. 

## Recommendations
- Core Revenue Drivers: Clothing ($102.97K) and Accessories ($73.48K) bring in 76.5% of all sales through high order volume. Keep top sellers (Blouses, Shirts, Dresses, Pants, Jewelry) in stock and featured on your homepage.
-Order Value vs. Volume: Footwear leads in Average Order Value ($60.23), while Clothing relies on frequent, smaller purchases. Bundle high-value Boots and Shoes with popular Clothing items to increase cart sizes.
- Weakest Performers: Outerwear makes up just 7.9% of sales ($18.4K), while Jeans ($7,548) and Jackets ($56.74 AOV) are the lowest-performing items. Run seasonal discounts or clearance promos on Jackets, Coats, and Jeans to boost demand.
- Size M Trends: Size M gets high order volume in top states, but buyers spend less per order ($52.66–$57.43). Add a $65 free-shipping minimum or multi-item discounts to encourage larger baskets.
- Size XL Trends: Size XL has low order counts, but buyers spend the most per order (up to 71.9 AOV). Target Size XL shoppers in top states (Nebraska, North Dakota, California) with focused ads and emails.
